Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026  ·  Applies to [YOUR_DOMAIN]

This Privacy Policy explains how [YOUR_APP_NAME] ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our freight email outreach platform.

1 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account data: email address, hashed password, account role, and invitation token used during registration.
  • Email account credentials: Gmail address and app password you connect to the platform. Passwords are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Usage data: records of outreach emails sent, replies received, follow-ups scheduled, and pipeline entries you create.
  • Session data: browser session tokens and CSRF tokens stored in secure, HTTP-only cookies.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

2 Gmail & Google API Access

🔒 Gmail Security — Key Points
  • Your Gmail credentials (address + app password) are encrypted with Fernet symmetric encryption before being stored in our database. The encryption key is held separately as an environment secret and is never committed to source code.
  • We access your Gmail account only to send outreach emails and check for replies on your behalf — no other actions are performed.
  • Your credentials and tokens are never shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party under any circumstances.
  • Gmail access uses SMTP/IMAP with your App Password, which can be revoked at any time directly from your Google Account settings without affecting your main Google password.
  • If you delete your account, all stored credentials are permanently removed from our database.

Our use of Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3 How We Store Your Data

  • Database: PostgreSQL hosted on Railway's secure infrastructure. All database connections use TLS.
  • Passwords: User account passwords are hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plaintext).
  • Gmail credentials: Encrypted with Fernet before database insertion; the encryption key is stored as a separate environment variable.
  • Session cookies: HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax, and Secure flags enabled in production.
  • Backups: Managed by Railway's database service. Backup data is subject to the same security controls.

4 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you and manage your session.
  • Send freight outreach emails from your connected Gmail account.
  • Retrieve and display replies to your outreach emails.
  • Schedule and send follow-up emails.
  • Maintain your pipeline and send history.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics resale, or any purpose beyond the core functionality described above.

5 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Railway (hosting & database): Your data is stored on Railway's infrastructure. Railway's own privacy policy governs their data handling.
  • Google (SMTP/IMAP): Emails are sent through Google's mail servers using your credentials. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that transmission.
  • Legal obligation: If required by law or valid legal process, we may disclose information as legally required.

6 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
  • Upon deletion: account record, email credentials, send/reply history, and pipeline data are permanently removed from our production database.
  • Residual data in database backups may persist for up to 30 days before being purged on backup rotation.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

8 Cookies

We use a single session cookie strictly necessary for authentication. We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. No third-party cookie consent banner is required.

9 Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
